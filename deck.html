Novelty & Guardrails

Why RoundsAI Wins & Stays Compliant

Purpose-built domain innovation designed strictly within ethical healthcare scope boundaries.

06 / 07

🏆 Core Technological Novelty

  • Hardware Harmony: Phone acts as the portable bedside sensor; computer serves as the high-density review station.
  • Zero-Cloud Audio Privacy & ₹0 Cost: On-device Whisper AI ensures sensitive patient voice never leaves the hospital firewall.
  • Oncology-Specific Normalizer: Tailored regex phonetics for 120+ chemotherapy regimens and lab counts.
  • Instant Bedside-to-Desktop Sync: Seamless background transmission without manual file sharing or email.

🛡️ Strict Scope Guardrail Compliance

  • Assistive Documentation Only: Does not provide autonomous diagnosis, clinical risk scoring, or unverified treatment orders.
  • Objective Data Presentation: Visualizes historical lab trends and indexes published guidelines for clinician reference.
  • Mandatory Clinician-in-the-Loop: The attending physician retains 100% clinical authority to review, edit, and sign every note.
  • Audit Provenance: Every extracted metric links back to its documented transcript or paper lab source.
